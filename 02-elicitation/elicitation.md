## Stakeholders:
  - Student
  - Lecturer
  - Technician
  - Equipment manager
## Stakeholder needs and concerns
  - ### Student:
      - Need: Know whether equipment is available
      - Concern: Being unable to get equipment when needed
  - ### Lecturer:
      - Need: Have equipment available for teaching
      - Concern: Equipment not available at required time
   - ### Technician:
      - Need: Know what equipment is due out and back
      - Concern: Late or missing equipment
   - ### Equipment manager:
      - Need: Maintain accountability for equipment 
      - Concern: Equipment being lost or used without authorisation
