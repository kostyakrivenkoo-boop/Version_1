# Week 5 — Activity Diagram
## 1. Process Modelled
  Selection of activity for volounteer
## 2. Purpose
  Show the step by step process of selecting activity, identify unkonws and desicions
## 3. Activities
  - Select Activity
  - Check for open registration
  - Check for available volounteer slot
  - Enter details
  - Submit Enrollment
  - Record enrollment
## 4. Decision and Guards
  - Is Registration open? (Open or closed)
  - Is there open slot for activity?(avilabe or unavailable)
## 5. Unknowns
  - What should happen if there is no free slots for volunteer?
  - What should happen if registration is close?
## 6. Stakeholder Question
  - What to display if there is no free slots for volunteer?(Ask organisation manager)
  - What to display if registration is close?(Ask organisation manager)
## 7. Modelling Decision
  Dependes on outcome of descicion e.g. there is a free slot for volounteer continue on with further steps of registration
## 8. Reflection
  In this Lab I learned how to use and create activity diagrams to identify each step of a procces, decisions and unknowns for task.
